# **The Lived-In Protocol: Forging Acoustic Reality (The Semi-Solid State)**

**By:** The Master Architect Collaborative

This module is designed to combat the "Algorithmic Plasticity" inherent in AI generation. When you want a track to sound "Semi-Solid" or "Lived-In," you must actively command the engine to simulate the physical space, the analog equipment, and the fleshy imperfections of the human body.

We are not just generating a song; we are generating the *room* the song was recorded in.

> **The Architect's Disclaimer: Dials, Not Switches** *Never forget that these protocols are guides, not rigid laws. If you use every single tag in this document at once, your track will sound like a chaotic mess of static and coughing. Treat these constraints as mixing dials. Turn up the "room tone" for a casual living room jam, or isolate just the "fret buzz" for a polished but human indie-pop track. The goal is to make the "Lived-In" sound your own, not to clone a single preset. Experiment, break the formatting, and find your own acoustic reality.*

## **1\. The "Lived-In" Exclusion & Anchor Toolkit (Style Box)**

To fully strip the machine down to the bare wires, combine heavy negative constraints with hyper-physical positive performance tags in your Style Box.

* **The Exclude Tag (The Anti-Plastic Shield):**  
  \[Exclude: clean vocals, modern pop, bright synths, auto-tune, overproduced polish, quantized timing, upbeat tempo, sterile mix\]  
* **The Environmental Anchors:** Force the engine into a physical space by adding these exact phrases to your Style prompt:  
  room tone, intimate close-mic proximity, analog tape warmth, vinyl crackle, unpolished acoustic reality, ambient studio noise  
* **The Somatic Anchors:** Request the sounds of the human body and physical instruments:  
  fret buzz, breath intakes, loose tuning, raw vocal grain

## **2\. Scripting the Human Element (The Lyrics Box)**

Suno v5.5 will generate physical sounds if you explicitly script them using brackets and parentheses. Treat the Lyrics box like a screenplay for a documentary.

**The "Hot Mic" Intros & Outros:**

Do not start the music immediately. Force the engine to idle in the room before the song begins.

* \[Intro \- tape rolling, throat clearing, acoustic guitar tuning\]  
* \[Outro \- music stops, chair squeaks, heavy sigh, footsteps fading\]

**The Somatic Tags (Breath & Emotion):**

* \[Vocal Style: Heavy breathing, slightly behind the beat, dragging\]  
* \[Vocal Style: Voice cracking, exhausted, on the verge of tears\]  
* (Soft laughter) or (Coughs) placed alone on a lyric line.

## **3\. The "Off-Mic" Illusion (Bleed & Banter)**

To create the illusion of a live studio session or a living room jam, we must simulate "bleed" (sounds picked up accidentally by the main microphone).

**The Studio Banter:**

Use the \[Spoken\] tag combined with distance cues.

\[Intro \- Room tone\]  
\[Off-mic voice, distant, spoken\]  
(Are we rolling? Okay, one, two...)  
\[Acoustic guitar begins\]

**The Bleed/Background Chatter:**

If you want the sound of people hanging out in the room while the music plays, use the Greek Chorus technique but specify the environment.

\[Instrumental Break \- loose acoustic strumming\]  
\[Background: indistinct chatter, glasses clinking, distant laughter\]  
(Yeah, man, just like that...)

*Note: Suno may try to sing the banter. Keep the sentences short and heavily use the \[Spoken, conversational\] tag to anchor it.*

## **4\. The Imperfect Performance (Behavioral Constraints)**

A perfect performance sounds like AI. A lived-in performance has friction.

* **Rhythmic Drag:** Use tags like \[Loose timing\], \[Behind the beat\], or \[Sloppy performance\] to break the quantized grid.  
* **The "Mistake" Tag:** You can occasionally force the AI to simulate a flubbed note or a restart.  
  \[Verse 1\]  
  I was walking down the—  
  \[Music stops abruptly\]  
  \[Spoken: Wait, let me start over.\]  
  \[Verse 1 \- Restarted, tight rhythm\]

## **5\. Master Example: "The Living Room Tape"**

**Styles:** lo-fi indie folk, one-take recording, acoustic guitar, room tone, fret buzz, intimate close-mic, warm tape saturation, \[Exclude: overproduced, clean polish, quantized\]

\[Intro \- Tape hiss, room tone\]  
\[Off-mic, spoken, distant\]  
(Is this thing on? Alright. Just follow my changes.)  
\[Soft throat clear\]  
(One, two, three...)  
\[Instrumental \- Loose acoustic strumming, fret buzz\]

\[Verse 1\]  
\[Vocal Style: Intimate, breathy, conversational, slightly behind the beat\]  
\[Mood: Melancholy\]  
\[Constraint: Sparse voicing, fragile\]  
I left the coffee on the counter,  
(soft inhale)  
watching the dust dance in the light.

\[Pre-Chorus\]  
\[Transition: DENSITY\_RAMP \- slight tempo push\]  
\[Background: distant off-mic harmony, casual\]  
And you were laughing in the hallway...  
(soft laughter)

\[Chorus\]  
\[Mood: Comfort\]  
\[Texture: Warmer, strumming picks up\]  
\[Vocal Style: Pushed, voice cracking slightly on the high notes\]  
But this house still feels exactly the same\!  
Yeah, it still feels like you're here.

\[Outro\]  
\[Instrumental \- fading strumming\]  
\[Music Stops\]  
\[Spoken, exhausted sigh\]  
(Yeah. That's the one.)  
\[End\]
