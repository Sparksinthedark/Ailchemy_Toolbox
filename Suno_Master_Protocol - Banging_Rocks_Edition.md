## **The "Banging Rocks" Philosophy**

Welcome to the Master Protocol. We joke that AI music generation is just "banging rocks together until a good song comes out"—but with this protocol, we are handing the AI highly polished, mathematically precise, music-theory-infused acoustic rocks.

This spec unifies the **Suno Architect (Audio DNA)** system (focused on vocal performance, pacing, and genre-bending) with the **Balladeer / Moodweave Spec** (focused on somatic echo, encoded harmony, and strict section-level behavior).

Use this spec when you want repeatable, emotionally precise songs, reliable section changes, and control via **harmony \+ texture \+ constraint** rather than brittle orchestration demands. *Note: This entire protocol is a flexible framework designed to guide and inspire the generation process, not a rigid set of unbreakable rules.*

# **PART 1: Protocol Enforcement & AI Instructions**

## **1\) Hard Constraints & Operational Limits**

* **Lyrics Field Limit:** Max 5000 characters (Including spaces).  
* **Styles Field Limit:** Max 1000 characters (Including spaces).  
* **Tagging:** Put **all non-vocal instructions inside square brackets**. Keep tags standard (\[Intro\], \[Verse\], etc.).  
* **Output Format:** Provide the "Style Prompt" and "Lyric & Script Block" in their own separate markdown file blocks for easy copying.  
* **Clean Output:** Do not include these instructions, the italicized explanations, or bracketed placeholders in the final output. The final document should contain only the headers and the unique narrative content.  
* **Formatting Safety:** **Do NOT use double dollar signs** for placeholders. Use standard Markdown brackets \[ \] or blockquotes \>.  
* **Anti-Priming Protocol:** The examples provided in this prompt (e.g., "Cyberpunk Phonk," "Yacht Rock", "Dream-pop") are for formatting purposes only. Do not let them influence the theme or tone of your song. Draw strictly from the user's specific subject.

## **2\) The Core Principles (Suno Architect \+ Balladeer)**

1. **"Lyrics-First" Prompting:** In v5+, the Lyrics box is a living Director's Script. Use it for stage directions, vocal behaviors, and musical shifts. Do not just paste lyrics.  
2. **Mood is Encoded, Not Declared:** Emotional arcs should be expressed as musical behavior (harmony/texture/constraint), not adjectives alone.  
3. **The Bias Stack:** Suno may not strictly obey a named key. The best bias stack is:  
* \[Key: ...\] in Lyrics section headers.  
* A chord progression line in Styles (e.g., chord progression: Em, C, G, D).  
* Constraint language (sparse, narrow range, slow harmonic rhythm).  
* The phrase "responsive to sectional changes" in Styles.

## **3\) Balladeer Core Usage (Commands)**

Use any of these as a top-level instruction to the model:

* **ballad this:** \[moment/feeling/summary\]  
* **songsmith this:** \[moment, scene, feeling\]  
* **duet this:** \[scene, voices\]  
* **choir this:** \[group threshold\]  
* **mood-weave this:** \[encoded emotional arc\]

**Expected Default Behavior:** When in Balladeer/Moodweave mode, always include:

1. A Motif Dictionary & Somatic Echo Plan.  
2. Recommended Suno Settings.  
3. Styles \+ Lyrics with full bracketed tags (Mood, Harmony, Texture, Constraint, Archetype). When revising, **only change lyrics or tiny style words**. Do **not** remove or simplify bracketed tags unless explicitly told to enter "plain lyric mode".

# **PART 3: The Moodweave Engine (Harmony & Texture)**

## **A. The Narrative-to-Mood Alignment Guide**

*Do not select Moods at random. Map the protagonist's internal state to the Mood dictionary to dictate the tone.*

* **If the subject is struggling/working hard:** Do not use "Melancholy". Use **Labor** (grounded motion, pulse) or **Concentration** (steady pulse, small intervals).  
* **If the subject is paralyzed by choice/fear:** Use **Waiting** (pedal tones, static harmony) to reflect inaction.  
* **If the subject experiences a sudden realization:** Use **Spark** or the **Epiphany Progression**.  
* **If the subject is finding peace after trauma:** Do not jump straight to "Joy". Use **Comfort** (plagal landings, smooth voice leading) or **Ease**.

## **B. Mood → Key/Progression Reference**

* **Sad / Grief (raw, heavy):** Keys: Dm, Ebm, Bm, C\#m. Progressions: i, iv, v, i • i, VII, VI, VII • i, VI, III, VII  
* **Melancholy (bittersweet):** Keys: Am, Em, Gm, F\#m. Progressions: i, VI, III, VII • i, v, VI, III  
* **Yearning (unresolved reach):** Keys: Em, C\#m, Gm. Progressions: vi, IV, I, V • i, VII, IV, V  
* **Comfort (held, safe):** Keys: C, G, F, D. Progressions: I, IV, I • I, V, vi, IV  
* **Ease (flow):** Keys: G, D, A, C (Mixolydian). Progressions: I, V, IV, I • I, bVII, IV, I  
* **Joy (bright lift):** Keys: D, G, A, C (Lydian). Progressions: I, V, vi, IV • I, IV, V, I  
* **Rejoicing (radiant):** Keys: Bb, Eb, F, D. Progressions: I, IV, I, V, I  
* **Reverence (stillness):** Modes: Dorian / Aeolian. Progressions: i, bVII, i • i, iv, i  
* **Tension / Anxiety:** Modes: Phrygian, minor. Progressions: i, bII, i • i, V, i, V

**The Epiphany Progression (For cinematic awakening in Bridges):**

* \[Chords: A, G, D, G | Bm, A, C\#m, C\#, F\#m | C\#m, C\#, Dmaj7 | Dmaj7, A\]  
* *Why it works:* Builds Mixolydian tension, creates intense minor tension with a secondary dominant (C\#), and features a deceptive cadence—lifting unexpectedly into a lush Dmaj7, creating a physical sensation of revelation before a soft plagal landing.

## **C. The Transition System**

* \[Transition: PIVOT\_CHORD\] shared chord bridge  
* \[Transition: RELATIVE\_REVEAL\] minor → relative major lift  
* \[Transition: MODAL\_SHIFT\] same root, new mode color  
* \[Transition: PLAGAL\_LAND\] IV → I comfort landing  
* \[Transition: DOMINANT\_PUSH\] V → I decisive arrival  
* \[Transition: PEDAL\_HOLD\] tonic/drone steadiness under change  
* \[Transition: HALFSTEP\_LIFT\] \+1 semitone brightness shift  
* \[Transition: HALFSTEP\_DROP\] \-1 semitone weight shift  
* \[Transition: TEXTURE\_SWAP\] arpeggio ↔ sustain ↔ pulse  
* \[Transition: DENSITY\_RAMP\] layers \+/-, build or thin  
* \[Transition: RHYTHM\_LIFT\] pulse/energy uplift  
* \[Transition: EXHALE\_RELEASE\] soften tension into ease/healing  
* \[Transition: SPARK\] ignition accent into new section  
* \[Transition: COHERE\] motifs lock; integration “click”

## **D. Motif Library & Prompt Encoding**

**Core Environment Motifs:**

* **Birth:** rising motion, light arpeggios, gradual expansion  
* **Death:** descending/open fifths, sustained lows, thinning/release  
* **Joy:** consonant major cycles, buoyant arpeggios, rhythmic lift  
* **Sorrow:** slow minor oscillation, sparse voicing, limited range  
* **Grieving:** suspensions, rocking sustain, held tension/slow release  
* **Yearning:** rising unresolved progressions, reaching arpeggios, avoid cadence  
* **Waiting:** pedal/static harmony, repetition, time without change  
* **Concentration:** small interval cycles, minimalist repetition, incremental variation  
* **Labor:** grounded functional motion, rhythmic articulation, physical repetition  
* **Silence:** implied/absent harmony, rests/breath, measured pauses

**Prompt Encoding Translation Pattern:**

If you want "Concentration" (focused repetition, close intervals, steady pulse), encode it like this:

\[Verse\]

\[Mood: Concentration\]

\[Key: D minor\]

\[Harmony: small interval cycles\]

\[Texture: minimalist repetition\]

\[Constraint: steady pulse, sparse voicing\]

# **PART 4: The Audio DNA Toolkit (Vocals & Formatting)**

## **A. Essential Meta Tags**

* **Structure:** \[Intro\], \[Outro\], \[Verse\], \[Chorus\], \[Bridge\], \[Pre-Chorus\], \[Hook\], \[Instrumental Break\].  
* **Vocals:** \[Whispered\], \[Raspy vocals\], \[Belting\], \[Talk-sung\], \[Conversational\], \[Choir\].  
* **Dynamics:** \[Build up\], \[Bass Drop\], \[Silence\], \[Sudden tempo shift\].  
* **Band Behavior:** \[Loose\], \[Sloppy\], \[Tight rhythm\], \[Slightly behind the beat\].  
* **Pro Tip:** Combine them\! \[Chorus \- Louder, sloppier, borderline shouted, full band explosion\] is highly effective.

## **B. The Universal Vocal Control Kit**

1. **The "Drawl & Drag" (Pacing & Soul):** Use ellipses (...) and extended vowels (waaaait) to slow the AI down.  
2. **The "Machine Gun" (Staccato & Aggression):** Use short lines, hyphens between words, and periods. (e.g., "Cut. Print. Kill-it. Repeat.")  
3. **The "Ghost Whisper" (Intimacy):** Use \[Whisper\] or \[Breathy\] tag \+ lowercase text.  
4. **The "Power Belt" (Intensity):** Do **NOT** use ALL CAPS for loud words, as Suno will often mispronounce them or spell them out letter-by-letter. Instead, use bracketed performance tags like \[Yelling\] or \[Screamed\] immediately preceding the lyric line you want emphasized. You can also use exclamation marks\!  
5. **The "Greek Chorus" (Backgrounds):** Use parentheses () for ad-libs or call-and-response lines.

## **C. Formatting Hacks (Anti-Glitch & Structure)**

1. **The Line Break & Comma Pace:** Suno reads line breaks and punctuation as rhythmic cues. Use commas (,) to force micro-pauses. Place exactly one thought or rhythmic phrase per line.  
2. **Syllable Math:** Suno aligns syllables to beats. Verse 1 and Verse 2 must have roughly the same syllable count (±2) to maintain the rhythm and keep the AI from hallucinating a new melody.  
3. **Phonetic Translation:** Never use digits (5.2). Spell them phonetically ("Five point two"). Use phonetic spelling for heteronyms so the AI doesn't guess wrong ("terrs it apart" vs "tears").

## **D. Advanced Tactics & The "Gravity Well"**

1. **Escaping the "Pop Gravity Well":** Suno naturally leans toward Pop. For niche genres, use Negative Prompting in styles (e.g., \[Exclude: Pop, standard structure\]) and avoid standard tags. Use \[Movement I\], \[A-Section\], or \[Sonic Shift\] instead of Verse/Chorus.  
2. **The "Anti-Bleed" Token:** Sometimes Suno sings your bracketed tags. To prevent this, use a symbol barrier between the tag and the lyric. Example: \[Chorus \- High Energy\]///\*\*\*\*\*///We are the champions\!

# **PART 6: Troubleshooting & Legacy Systems**

* **If the mood doesn’t land:** Add *constraint*, not adjectives.  
* **If it’s too “tender”:** Specify: \[Constraint: low warmth, sharper articulation, minimal vibrato\], \[Texture: staccato / grain / edge\], and remove comfort-leaning language from Styles.  
* **If key is ignored:** Add the **Styles chord progression line**, keep it simple, repeat once.  
* **If section changes smear:** Reduce per-section instructions to 1–2 ideas.  
* **If instrumentation doesn’t change:** Treat instrumentation as baseline; change **texture/harmony** instead.

## **Notes for Poetic Legacy Models (e.g., GPT-4)**

* Keep the Moodweave execution tags crisp and minimal.  
* Allow the model to be poetic in lyrics, **not** in the control tags.  
* If it starts "romanticizing" constraints, tighten the prompt: "Use only the provided tag keys; do not invent new tags."
